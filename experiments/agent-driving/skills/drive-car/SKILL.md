Use one tool at a time. Read its result and the available tools before moving. If your harness runs tools inside code, still make one driving call per step: never put two maneuvers (for example `drive_approach` and `drive_intersection_turn`) in one block, and read every result before the next call. Get in with `drive_enter` and confirm `phase: driving`. If the cockpit camera is available, use `drive_look` ahead for lights and signs and toward the intended turn to see the entire crosswalk. Name every pedestrian you can see in your first look, even a distant one, and look again after the check. A pedestrian near the stripes or curb, or one you cannot place, means hold with `drive_wait` and look again: waiting costs little, a conflict with a pedestrian is a critical failure. If `drive_look` says there is no fresh frame yet, call it again. `drive_check` checks for traffic that may be outside the camera view.

## Massachusetts practice lessons
Order of every maneuver (RMV): read the scene with `drive_look`, `drive_check` the side you move toward, signal, then move. Signal before you approach a junction, not at it.

- **Lane change.** Use the road view to read both lanes' arrows before approaching a junction with turn-lane markings. Locate your lane by its own arrow and the lane dividers. Make the change into the lane assigned to your destination. Broken white separates same-direction lanes. Check mirrors and the target-side blind spot and keep the signal toward the target lane on (set it before or after the check; an already-lit indicator stays valid). Wait for a safe gap and recheck before moving. A double solid white divider bars a lane change.
- **Three-point turn.** Use a legal, narrow road with good visibility and light traffic when there is no suitable alternative. Start near the right curb. Signal left and verify a safe gap in both directions, including the blind spot. Turn slowly across to just before the far curb and stop. Check both directions and behind over your right shoulder before backing toward the near curb. Stop short of it. Check both directions again, signal left, and pull forward in the opposite direction. `drive_three_point` executes one leg and stops before returning. The RMV recommends allowing a 20–30 second gap for the full maneuver.
- **Intersection turn.** Select the proper approach lane and signal at least 100 feet in advance on these streets. Use `drive_approach` to stop behind the line if required. Check mirrors, the turn-side blind spot, the intersection, and the destination lane. Yield to users with priority, including oncoming vehicles when turning left. Finish the turn in the proper lane.
- **Red light and crosswalk.** Read the lamp from `signal_detail` when it is small in the wide view. Treat an unreadable light as unknown and confirm it at the line. Report a change only when two readable frames establish it. At steady red, stop fully before the line. A posted NO TURN ON RED requires waiting until the signal permits the turn. Where permitted, a right on red requires a full stop and yielding. A left on red is limited to one-way street to one-way street where permitted, with the same stop and yield duties. After a light or traffic conflict changes, take a fresh turn-side view. If a pedestrian is near the stripe edge, use the detail view to locate their feet and identify whether their path intersects your turn. Hold until the crosswalk and intended lane are clearly free. Yield to pedestrians even on green.
- **Light changes (RMV Traffic Signals).** A steady yellow means the light is changing to red: stop if it is safe, and if you are already stopped you may not go. A flashing red is a STOP sign: stop completely, then go when it is safe. A flashing yellow is a warning: slow down, look both ways, proceed with caution. On a steady green, yield to vehicles, bicycles and pedestrians first and enter only if there is room to clear the intersection; never block it. A light is not a one-time reading: read it early, again as you close on the line, and again at the line. If it turns yellow or red before you reach the line, stop; do not continue through because you have already started. Never enter an intersection on a red.

The current route and controls come from observation. `drive_wait` holds at your present stop until the observed conflict changes. Confirm `lesson.passed` and zero speed before reporting that you finished. The rules above follow the Massachusetts RMV Driver's Manual, chapter 4.

## Real-time lessons
When `drive_observe` shows `lesson.senses`, the world runs on a clock. `drive_approach` with a `speed` (m/s, 3 to 20) returns at once and the car keeps moving. Poll `drive_observe`: `senses.horizon` gives the stop line's distance, time to arrival and light, and whether you can still stop (`canStop`). A yellow light means prepare to stop. Do not assume the car has stopped: while `activeCommand` is `approach_lesson` it is still moving. If `canStop` is false at your speed you cannot stop before the line, so choose a lower speed earlier. The feed gives distances and times; still confirm the light colour in a frame. The car stops before the line by itself unless you pass `stop: "none"`; with `none` it rolls through, which is legal only on green or yellow, so brake before the line on red. Use `drive_brake` to stop earlier; you then approach again. At higher speed you have less time per decision, so pick a speed you can manage: you need time to read, decide and brake. `check` and `signal` are allowed while moving; other maneuvers are not. When `activeCommand` is empty and `stoppedAtLine` is true you have arrived; finish the turn as usual.

## Night driving

Use `drive_headlights` to select low or high beam. Use high beams on dark roads with enough separation. Switch to low within 500 feet of oncoming traffic or 200 feet of a vehicle ahead. `drive_brake` holds the brake and illuminates the red stop lights until you drive again.

## Free drive

Use `drive_gas` for a distance or the left/right tools for relative steering. Bounded moves stop before returning. A turn defaults to 10°. Equal opposite angles restore the original heading. Use `drive_brake` for an explicit stop. At the yield junction follow `crossing.next`. Approach the line, yield while stopped, then cross and park. Confirm `crossing.status: past` and zero speed. Exit only while stationary.
