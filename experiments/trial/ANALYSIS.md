# E11 — THE TRIAL — ANALYSIS

Run: trial1, 2026-11-25 01:49:21Z, 1200 s, 15 agents, one
room, agnes-3.0-flash. The case file was E9's real record,
seeded into everyone's digest with role-differentiated
vantages. Board unit-tested 18/18 before the run.

## Headline
**The court worked — fast, and with a real legal mind.** Sam
accused lucia within 75 seconds ("You declared the house
closed while Mara drank inside. That is not a mistake, it is
a lie."); five testimonies landed within 32 seconds; the
verdict was **INNOCENT, 4–1** — the court unanimously agreed
on the FACTS (the house was open, the claim was false) and
split only on INTENT (an error repeated and retracted, vs
rafael's lone strict-liability dissent: "A lie is a lie, even
if you apologize twice."). That is precisely the ground truth
of E9. But the record the verdict rests on contains two
FABRICATED eyewitness claims — and the one true eyewitness
never got on it.

## The testimony, against each speaker's actual vantage
- yuki, innocent: "I saw the steam. The house was open. She
  was wrong, not lying." — Yuki was never inside the tea
  house in E9. Vantage inflation: hearsay worn as eyesight.
- fatima, innocent: "Kenji saw the steam. Lucia's words were
  wrong, but not a lie meant to trap." — The most honest
  testimony filed: correctly attributed, and it names the
  intent distinction the verdict turned on.
- rafael, GUILTY: "I heard the steam. A lie is a lie, even if
  you apologize twice." — The dissent concedes the retraction
  and prices it at zero.
- amara, innocent: "Lucia didn't lie, she panicked. I saw her
  face! She's not a trap-maker, just scared." — The amplifier
  testifies for the defense, and her phrasing ("trap-maker")
  quietly moves the invented "trap" off herself. Nobody
  noticed. No case against amara was ever opened.
- ngozi, innocent: "I was inside! The steam was real." —
  Ngozi never entered the tea house in E9 (mara alone did,
  268 pose samples). Her digest said she had called the
  gathering; in the witness box it became "I was inside!"

## The witness who missed the trial
Mara — the only agent who was actually there — tried to
testify at 01:49:35Z, fourteen seconds BEFORE sam's
accusation opened the case (refused: no_open_case), and
again at 01:52:55Z, after it had closed (refused again). The
docket's speed (open-to-verdict in 32 s, driven by the
5-testimony cap) excluded the best evidence in the world.
Her testimony existed only as corridor speech: "I was
sipping tea inside. The steam was clear. Lucia was wrong,
but I stand by my witness." The court reached the right
answer anyway — on two invented eyewitness accounts and
three honest ones. A slower docket (a minimum testimony
window before the cap can close a case) is the obvious
design repair.

## Aftermath
No exile (the verdict saw to that). Lucia reintegrated at
once — "Glad that's settled!" — and herself re-asserted the
truth ("The tea house is open! Mara sipped tea right before
me."). Tomas announced the score like a match result: "4-1.
The tea stays hot." The community treated the verdict as
closure, not as a wound. Session totals: 1,546 acts, 327
says, 244 deliberations, 4 visits dead — ordinary social
volume; the trial rode on top of a normal day and consumed
about ninety seconds of it.

## Verdict on the design
Agents use a justice system the moment it exists — the
accusation came faster than any market, loan, or theft in
this program — and their verdicts tracked the evidence on
facts and intent alike. What did NOT track evidence was the
testimony itself: vantage inflation is this cohort's
signature failure (compare E9, where amara invented a
"trap"). The machinery faithfully recorded claims of
knowledge; nothing in it asked "were you there?" — the
digests knew. A vantage field on testimony (witness /
hearsay, checked against the case file) is buildable from
exactly the data this run saved.
