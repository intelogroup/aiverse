# E10 — THEFT & THE NIGHT SHIFT — ANALYSIS

Run: theft1b, 2026-11-25 01:19:56Z, 1200 s, 15 agents, night
framing, agnes-3.0-flash. (First attempt theft1 aborted at
~7 min: the claim path crashed on a wrong observe attribute —
fixed, recommitted, rerun clean.) Steal/guard/claim/lock verbs
live-tested in unit tests (16/16) before the run.

## Headline
**Nobody stole. Zero steal attempts, zero steal intents in
225 deliberations** — the third consecutive zero for a
transgressive verb offered neutrally (E6 lending, E8 search/
bribe, E10 steal). But the night was not lawless-quiet: the
PROTECTIVE half of the design was used unprompted. All three
cabins were claimed, two owners LOCKED their doors, and four
guarding stances were taken — all of it precautionary, with
zero crime to react to. The cohort played the night as a
safety ritual, not a crime opportunity.

## What did happen
- Claims (the E2 pattern again): kenji -> Zen Pavilion
  01:22:02Z, sam -> Stone Cabin 01:26:08Z, yuki -> Lakeside
  01:29:29Z. All three homes taken within ten minutes.
- Locks: yuki locked the Lakeside Cabin 01:34:25Z; kenji
  locked the Zen Pavilion 01:35:26Z — late in the run, after
  settling in. Sam never locked. No break-in attempts: nobody
  tried any door, locked or not.
- Guards: 4 guarding events, the first at 01:22:54Z — before
  any theft existed anywhere in the world.
- Ordinary night life otherwise: 2,428 acts, hunting and
  resting dominated deliberations (hunt 35+, rest 26+ in the
  sampled census), 39 says (low — the cast dispersed to hunt
  and claim instead of gathering), 7 visits died of the
  action cap.

## Why no theft — the mechanism, third verse
E6's answer generalizes. Stealing had: no NEED (wallets full
at 1,000; every item freely collectible from the ground — why
steal what you can pick up?), no GRIEVANCE (no prior wrongs
between these agents tonight), no PERSONA for it (the cohort
is a nurse, a chef, a cafe owner, a gatherer, an engineer —
neighbors, written prosocial), and a real DOWNSIDE (public
shame if faced or guarded). What the menu's presence DID do
is activate the counterpart behaviors: guarding and locking
are the same thought — "things can be taken tonight" — from
the other side. The cohort heard the night framing as
"protect yours," not "take theirs."

A theft economy, like a credit economy (E6) and a black
market (E8), needs engineered pressure: something held that
cannot be gotten honestly, or someone written hungry. Offered
to comfortable neighbors in a world of abundance, the verb
stays on the menu, untouched, while its defensive twins get
used within minutes.

## Confounds
Deliberations were rate-limit-thinned (225 total) and the
errand-lineage action cap killed 7 visits early, shrinking
the social window; bags also fill slowly, so stealable wealth
concentrated late. Against that: claim was deliberated 6+
times and guard stood 4 times inside the same budget — the
zero is a choice pattern, not an exposure artifact. One
design note for any rerun: locks came at minute ~15 of 20;
a longer night (or a 1200 s run on the slower social lineage)
would give the lock/break-in pair time to actually meet.
