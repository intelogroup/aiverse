# City 3 placeholder audit: close-up survey (2026-11-06)

Read-only survey. Nothing in the code, GLBs or `tools/city3` was changed. 54 shots taken in the real console (`?world=city3&districtDebug=1`, own vite on :5191,
SwiftShader, 1000x640) at street level (1.7-3 m) and 5-8 m, across all 5 districts, the 4 bridges and one hero interior. Every shot was looked at.
Shots live in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-aiverse/8cbe7067-28c2-5d6b-99b8-5e8eadd47425/scratchpad/audit/` (`bz*` Bazaar, `gq*` Glass Quarter, `fw*` Foundry, `tr*` Terraces,
`ha*` Halo, `br*` bridges); harness `ashot.mjs` + `b1..b5.json` in the same folder (ESM ignores NODE_PATH: symlink `node_modules/playwright` into the audit dir).
Already known and NOT repeated as findings: bottles (done), hero doors on the 77 shops (done), trees (another lane), interior placeholders in doc 12.

Scale of the problem: the Bazaar and Halo already read as designed places. Glass Quarter, Foundry and Terraces read as *empty plans*: huge flat expanses, one furniture
set stamped on a grid, and (Glass/Terraces) shopfronts that are boxes behind glass. The biggest gap per district is not a single prop, it is missing *life*: vehicles, goods, people-scale clutter.

## Ranked top 15 by visible impact (impact per cost in mind)
1. **Empty streets: no vehicles/people anywhere except Halo** (G2, F4, L1). Reuse Halo `car()` on Glass avenue, Foundry main street, Terraces avenue and all four bridges; instanced idle figures from the Summer rig. Procedural, existing code, ~0 licence.
2. **Bazaar stall goods = coloured cuboids** (B1). Avramania fruit/sack/basket/roll (CC0) or procedural icosphere produce; every stall, most visited spot.
3. **Garden Terraces garden blocks = vast flat plane with pastel crystals** (T1). Grass texture, flower beds, hedges, pergola arches.
4. **Glass Quarter podium shops show empty boxes behind glass** (G1). Shelf/fixture strips or emissive display atlas.
5. **Foundry loft loading bays are flat rust rectangles** (F1). Procedural roller doors, 6 variants (also fulfils doc 10).
6. **Foundry shipping containers are Lego bricks** (F2). Container v2 with corner castings, lock rods, ribs.
7. **Foundry has no loose industrial props** (F4). Forklift, pallets, drums, hand trucks (CC-BY/CC0 list above).
8. **Terraces villas have no entrance** (T2). Porch, step, gate, `doors.py` styles.
9. **Glass Quarter plaza and park are 54 m flat planes** (G4). Fountain, statues (PH bronze statues already local), flower beds.
10. **Floating green quads over Halo (verify)** (H4). Bug, not an asset.
11. **Street furniture set is the same on 4 districts** (G3, F5, B6, B7): benches = slabs, bollards = sticks, bins = cans. CC-BY bench/bollard/bin swap, ~2k tris total, instanced.
12. **Quays with no maritime content: Foundry, Terraces, Bazaar** (F6, T5). Row boats, fishing boat, mooring bollards, rope, ladders.
13. **Cone-shaped planter shrubs and pastel shrub blobs** (B5, H3, T1): swap to Avramania `Bush`/leaf-card bushes (CC0).
14. **Roof clutter missing everywhere** (B8, G6, T6): chimneys, tanks, AC, PV. Procedural + `Overhead Water tank`, `Rooftop Ventilations`.
15. **Campus plaza terrazzo is a candy-colour mosaic** (G5): re-tint the texture. One-line fix, high visibility on the east side of Glass.
